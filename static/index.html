Stable Color Promise

Once you see a color, it won't change for the next 8 hours. This means you can confidently plan your energy usage without worrying about colors shifting.

Smart scheduling: Use green hours for dishwashers, washing machines, electric car charging, and other energy-intensive tasks.

How It Works

Energy Pebble fetches real-time electricity prices from Elia's grid data and converts them into simple color codes to help you optimize your energy consumption. Signed-in users tune the signal to their household (energy contract, solar panels, home battery), and their pebble follows automatically.
