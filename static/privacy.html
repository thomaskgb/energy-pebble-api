What we collect

If you have an account

Your username and password, the language you chose for this website, and the profile that drives your pebble: your contract type, whether you have solar panels or a home battery, and your display preferences. If you name your homes, we store those names and any address you type.

If you have a pebble

Each pebble identifies itself when it asks for colours. We store its device identifier, the IP address it connected from, the software it reports, how many times it has asked, and when we last heard from it. This is what lets us pair a pebble to your household during setup.

If you joined the waitlist

Your email address, the date you left it, and which language you were reading. Nothing else. We use it once, to tell you when pebbles are available.

What we do not collect

We do not know what you use electricity for, or how much. Your pebble reports that it asked for a colour, not what you switched on. There is no advertising, no tracking pixels and no analytics on this site, and we do not sell or share anything with anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Device records are deleted after twelve months without a single connection. A pebble that comes back after that is simply a new pebble, and pairs again by scanning its sticker.
  • Your account and its settings are kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Waitlist addresses are kept until you ask us to remove yours, or until we have told you pebbles are available and you have not ordered one.

Where it is stored

On a server we rent from DigitalOcean in Amsterdam, in the Netherlands. Your data stays in the European Union and is not transferred outside it.

Two outside services are involved in producing the colours, and neither receives anything about you: electricity prices come from Elia, the Belgian grid operator, and the sunshine forecast used for solar households comes from Open-Meteo. We ask both the same question no matter who is asking.

Cookies

If you sign in, a session cookie keeps you signed in. If you pick a language while signed out, that choice is remembered in your own browser and never reaches us. There are no other cookies, and none of them are used for tracking or advertising.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or take it elsewhere. Email us and we will do it. We do not charge for this and we do not need a reason.

There is no delete button yet, so account deletion is done by hand: write to energypebble@tdlx.nl and we will remove your account, your homes, your settings and your device claims.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority at gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so here and update the date at the top. We will not quietly start collecting something new.
